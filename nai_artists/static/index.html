    Add artists

    Adds matrix rows, nothing else: no image is generated here. Every cell of a new row starts missing; click its ⟳ to generate that one image.

      Battery

      …

      The gauge is coarse (one image rarely moves it). Rough budget: ~18 images per 1 %.

      Queue

      Live in the drawer (q). Every click on a cell's ⟳ queues that one image; the queue sends them one at a time with a short gap, and checks the battery guard. Both come from settings.toml.

      Import

      drop PNGs here, or

      filestatustemplate × artistsparamsdetails

      Unmatched files land in data/inbox/ untouched. Wrong seed / size is accepted and shown as ≠. A file without artist: tokens is the baseline cell of its template.

      Templates

      Files in . Toggles rewrite the front matter only; the prompt body stays byte-exact (hash unchanged). Edit bodies in your editor. New templates come from images: import results or the lightbox.
